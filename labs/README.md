# Lab sequence

This folder contains a five-lab teaching sequence built from the tutorial notebooks.

| Lab | Topic | Main idea |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | [Waves in a Box: Gravity Waves and Seiches](01_waves_in_a_box.md) | A small bump on the water surface sends waves across a rectangular basin. |
| 2 | [Tsunami Across the Ocean: Speed, Shoaling, and Sponge Layers](02_tsunami_shoaling.md) / [svenska](02_tsunami_shoaling_sv.md) | A tsunami can be low in the deep ocean but grow near the coast as the water becomes shallow. |
| 3 | [Wind, Storms, and Coastal Sea Level](03_wind_storms_and_coasts.md) | Wind can push water across the ocean, pile it against a coast during a storm, and launch sea-level signals that travel along boundaries. |
| 4 | [Rotation Changes Everything: Geostrophic Adjustment and Rossby Waves](04_rotation_geostrophy_rossby.md) | On a rotating planet, water does not simply flow straight downhill. |
| 5 | [Ocean Pathways: Gyres, Tides, and Abyssal Flow](05_ocean_pathways.md) | Large-scale ocean pathways can be shaped by wind, tides, sources and sinks, and density-layer effects. |

The guided lab notebooks live directly in `notebooks/`. The original tutorial notebooks have been converted to scripts in `scripts/`; running them writes generated movies to `animations/`.
