# Lab 01 - Waves in a Box: Gravity Waves and Seiches

**Based on:** `02_gravity_waves.ipynb and 06_seiche.ipynb`

## Story

A small bump on the water surface sends waves across a rectangular basin. When the waves reflect from the walls, the basin can slosh at special natural periods called seiches.

## Learning goals

- Gravity waves travel faster in deeper water.
- Closed basins reflect waves and can form standing waves.
- A simple theory can predict the period of a basin seiche.

## Guiding questions

- How does the wave speed change when depth changes?
- What does a standing wave look like?
- Can you measure the period from the model output?

## Suggested experiment

1. Run a Gaussian bump in a closed basin.
2. Estimate wave speed from the animation or a Hovmoller diagram.
3. Run a seiche initial condition and compare the measured period with theory.

## Teacher notes

Keep the first run small enough to execute quickly. For outreach use, emphasize the animation first and the equations second. For older students, ask for one measured quantity and one comparison with theory.
